# Drumstr Vision

## Problem

Music and rhythm are fundamental to human connection, but access to regular, facilitated group drumming is limited by:
- **Geography** — you can only drum with people nearby
- **Schedule** — classes are at fixed times, not on-demand
- **Cost** — private lessons and workshops are expensive
- **Expertise** — finding experienced facilitators is hard

## Solution

Drumstr is a **community-owned platform** for real-time, on-demand facilitated drumming sessions.

### Key Features
- **10 minutes per session** — intentionally short for daily engagement
- **Free to join** — optional sliding-scale Lightning payment after to support facilitators
- **Bitcoin-native** — payments via Lightning Network (instant, low-cost, self-custodial)
- **Nostr-native** — decentralized identity, no passwords, no surveillance
- **Open source** — MIT license, fork and build your own version

### How It Works
1. **Join a Drum Block** — Click a link at the scheduled time
2. **Drum Together** — 10 minutes of collective drumming via video/audio
3. **Optional Tip** — Support the facilitator with a Lightning payment (sliding scale: 0 sats → ∞)
4. **Come Back** — Do it again tomorrow

## Facilitator Economy

Drumstr is built around **fair compensation for facilitators**:

- **80% of tips** go directly to facilitators (via Lightning)
- **20% goes to DFE** (platform maintenance, development)
- **Sliding scale** — participants decide how much to tip (0 sats → ∞)
- **No minimum** — free to join, free to tip
- **Instant settlement** — Lightning payments settle in seconds

### Facilitator Benefits
- Earn income from home
- Flexible schedule (you choose which Blocks to facilitate)
- Build community (repeat participants, regular schedule)
- No platform fees (besides the 20% split)

### Participant Benefits
- Free drumming access
- Optional tips (you decide the amount)
- Anonymous participation (Nostr identity)
- Global community (drum with anyone, anywhere)
