## 1. BRAND ESSENCE

### Purpose
Drumstr is a decentralized virtual group drumming platform that unites people through rhythm, community, and the Bitcoin/Nostr ecosystem. We help people **Exhilarate, Transcend, and Belong** through the universal language of drumming.

### Mission
To create a global, self-sovereign community where rhythm connects us, Bitcoin rewards us, and Nostr gives us the freedom to be ourselves.

### Vision
A world where every person can tap into a global rhythm network, find their circle, and experience the transformative power of group drumming — without intermediaries, without surveillance, without borders.

### Core Values
1. **Rhythm as Freedom** — Drumming is primal expression. Our platform protects that freedom.
2. **Decentralized Community** — No gatekeepers. No algorithms deciding who belongs.
3. **Earned Connection** — Belonging is earned through participation, not purchased.
4. **Professional Stewardship** — Facilitators are trained leaders who earn their role.
5. **Radical Simplicity** — If it's not simple, it's not Drumstr.

- **RGB:** 210, 179, 86
- **Usage:** Primary CTAs, brand accents, highlights
- **Feeling:** Warmth, energy, value, tribal connection

#### Secondary: Deep Brown
- **Hex:** `#2c1810`
- **RGB:** 44, 24, 16
- **Usage:** Headlines, body text, footer backgrounds
- **Feeling:** Earth, grounding, stability, wood drums

#### Accent: Amber
- **Hex:** `#f5a623`
- **RGB:** 245, 166, 35
- **Usage:** Hover states, warnings, energy bursts
- **Feeling:** Excitement, fire, momentum

#### Neutral: Cream
- **Hex:** `#faf8f2`
- **RGB:** 250, 248, 242
- **Usage:** Page backgrounds, cards
- **Feeling:** Clean, warm, inviting

#### Support Colors
- **Gold Light:** `#e8d48b` — highlights, gradients
- **Gold Dark:** `#b8963e` — hover states, borders
- **Brown Light:** `#4a3728` — secondary text, subtle elements
- **Text Light:** `#6b5a4e` — body text on light backgrounds
- **Text Muted:** `#8b7b6e` — captions, placeholders
- **Border:** `#e0d5c8` — dividers, card borders

### Typography

#### Headlines: Fredoka
- **Weights:** 600, 700
- **Usage:** Hero headlines, section titles, step numbers
- **Feeling:** Friendly, bold, slightly playful — matches the joy of drumming
- **Source:** Google Fonts

#### Body: Inter
- **Weights:** 400, 500, 600
- **Usage:** Paragraphs, navigation, buttons, labels
- **Feeling:** Clean, modern, highly readable
- **Source:** Google Fonts

### Logo Concept
- **Primary Mark:** Stylized drum viewed from above with radiating sound waves
- **Colors:** DFE Gold gradient (`#e8d48b` → `#d2b356`)
- **Background:** Deep Brown circle (`#2c1810`)
- **Drum Body:** Brown ellipse with gold rim
- **Sound Waves:** Three radiating lines to the upper-right (energy in motion)
- **Usage:** Full color on light backgrounds; gold-only on dark backgrounds
- **Clear Space:** Minimum padding equal to 25% of logo diameter on all sides
- **Minimum Size:** 32px for digital, 12mm for print

### Iconography
- **Style:** Flat, slightly rounded, consistent stroke weight
- **Color:** DFE Gold or Deep Brown
- **Motifs:** Drums, rhythmic waves, circles, lightning bolts (Zaps), community gatherings

### Imagery Style
- **Photography:** Warm-toned, authentic community moments, diverse groups, real drumming
- **AI Generation:** Bold, cinematic, golden-hour lighting, energy in motion, tribal-modern fusion
- **Avoid:** Stock-looking images, cold tones, isolated individuals, corporate settings

## 4. DESIGN PRINCIPLES

### 4.1 Energy Flow
Every page should guide the eye in a rhythmic pattern — like a drumbeat. Use hierarchy, color, and spacing to create visual rhythm.

### 4.2 Warmth First
Before a user reads a word, they should *feel* the warmth. Cream backgrounds, gold accents, generous spacing.

### 4.3 Confidence Without Arrogance
We're decentralized leaders, not rebels without a cause. Design should feel established, professional, and trustworthy.

### 4.4 Simplicity as Feature
If a section doesn't earn its space, remove it. White space is our friend. Clarity is our brand.

### 4.5 Mobile as Primary
Design for the phone first. The thumb zone is sacred. CTAs must be reachable and obvious.

## 8. TECHNICAL BRAND CONTEXT

### Stack
- **Network:** Nostr (decentralized social/identity)
- **Payments:** Bitcoin Lightning (Zaps)
- **Audio:** MiroTalk (WebRTC-based)
- **Frontend:** Static HTML/CSS/JS (landing), React (app)
- **Backend:** PrivaFlow workflows + Directus

### API/Integration Branding
When integrating with external services, maintain Drumstr's visual identity in all user-facing touchpoints. Never let another brand's UI take over.

## 9. BRAND GUARDRAILS

### Do
- Use DFE Gold as the primary accent
- Keep headlines in Fredoka, body in Inter
- Lead with emotion, follow with clarity
- Frame facilitators as professionals
- Emphasize the demand-driven model's empowerment

### Don't
- Introduce cold blues or corporate grays as primary colors
- Use "volunteer" language for facilitators
- Oversimplify the demand-driven model
- Mix multiple font families beyond Fredoka + Inter
- Use stock photography that feels impersonal
