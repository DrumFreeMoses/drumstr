## OPTION 1: Centered Drum (Classic)

![Logo 1](logos/logo-01-centered-drum.svg)

### Description
A traditional drum viewed from a slight angle, centered in a gold circular badge with radiating sound waves. The drum has a wooden body with a golden drumhead. Sound waves emanate symmetrically to both sides.

### Personality
- **Classic** — instantly recognizable as a drum
- **Warm** — the gold badge feels premium and inviting
- **Balanced** — symmetrical composition conveys stability
- **Community-ready** — works well as a social avatar

### Best For
- Social media profiles (Nostr, Twitter)
- App icon
- Merchandise (stickers, t-shirts)
- Print materials

### Variations to Explore
- Monochrome version (gold on transparent)
- Dark mode version (light gold on dark brown)
- Animated version (sound waves pulsing)

## OPTION 2: Rhythm Rings (Modern)

![Logo 2](logos/logo-02-rhythm-rings.svg)

### Description
A top-down view of a drum surrounded by concentric rings suggesting sound waves propagating outward. The rings fade in opacity as they expand, creating a sense of rhythm and energy. Transparent background for maximum versatility.

### Personality
- **Modern** — abstract and geometric
- **Energetic** — the rings suggest motion and sound
- **Minimal** — clean lines, no unnecessary detail
- **Tech-forward** — fits the decentralized/Nostr ethos

### Best For
- Website favicon
- Loading animations
- UI elements (buttons, icons)
- Documentation headers

### Variations to Explore
- Single ring version (simplified)
- Animated pulse version
- Gradient ring versions (different brand colors)

## RECOMMENDATION

### Primary Logo: Option 2 (Rhythm Rings)
**Rationale:**
1. Most versatile — works on any background
2. Best performance at small sizes (favicon, app icon)
3. Aligns with the decentralized/tech-forward brand identity
4. The concentric rings subtly suggest the "Drum up a Circle" demand-driven model
5. Easy to animate for digital experiences

### Secondary/Support: Option 1 (Centered Drum)
**Rationale:**
1. Instant recognition — people know it's a drum
2. Strong presence for social media and merchandise
3. Provides a "human" counterpart to the abstract primary logo

### Tertiary: Option 3 (Monogram D)
**Rationale:**
1. Subtle brand presence for watermarks
2. Professional contexts where a full logo would be too much
3. Potential for wordmark integration

## PRODUCTION SPECS (Once Selected)

### File Formats
- **SVG:** Primary source format, scalable, editable
- **PNG:** 512x512, 256x256, 128x128, 64x64, 32x32 (transparent)
- **ICO:** 32x32, 16x16 (favicon)
- **WebP:** Optimized for web, with JPEG fallback

### Clear Space
- Minimum padding around logo: 25% of logo diameter on all sides

### Minimum Size
- Digital: 32px height
- Print: 12mm height

### Placement Rules
- Don't stretch or distort
- Don't change colors outside brand palette
- Don't add effects (drop shadows, outlines) unless specified
- Don't place on busy backgrounds without a container
