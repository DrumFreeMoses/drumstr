## PROMPT ENGINEERING NOTES

### Color Anchors
- **Primary:** DFE Gold `#d2b356` — must be dominant
- **Secondary:** Deep Brown `#2c1810` — grounding, shadows
- **Accent:** Amber `#f5a623` — energy bursts, highlights
- **Background:** Cream `#faf8f2` — warmth, not sterile white

### Mood Keywords
- Cinematic, golden-hour, tribal-modern, energy-in-motion
- Diverse, global, community, unity-in-rhythm
- Primal yet refined, ancient-yet-future

### Composition Rules
- Horizontal 16:9 aspect ratio for landing page hero
- Central focal point (drum or energy source)
- Surrounding figures/circle suggesting community
- Negative space in upper third for headline overlay

### Technical Specs
- Resolution: Minimum 2560x1440 for retina displays
- Format: WebP with JPEG fallback
- File size target: < 500KB optimized
