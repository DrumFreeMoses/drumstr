How It Works

The world's first demand-driven drumming network. You drum. The circle forms.

1

Drum Call

Tap your phone in the Drumstr app to send a rhythmic call across the Nostr network. Each drum call is a real-time signal—amplified by Lightning Zaps.

2

Hear & Respond

Other members hear your call in real time and respond with their own rhythms. The volume grows. The energy builds. The circle forms.

3

Threshold Met

When both quotas are reached—house minimum and personal minimum—the system dispatches a Facilitator Request automatically.

4

Session Begins

A trained facilitator claims the call. The session starts—or a delay time is published. You drum. You belong.
